Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Blocks Boom app (herein referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Josep as a Free service.

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects and stores data locally on your device (such as game progress, gold, and heroes) using a local database. This data is not transmitted to any external servers by the Application.

The Application does not require you to provide any personally identifiable information (PII) to function.

Third Party Access

The Application does not share any data with third parties. Since the Application is distributed via the Google Play Store, Google may collect certain information as described in their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

All game data is stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the Application, this local data will be deleted unless backed up by your operating system's standard cloud backup features (e.g., Google Play Games Cloud Save).

Children’s Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

Security

I value your trust in providing us your personal information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and I cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. I will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
